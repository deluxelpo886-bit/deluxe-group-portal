# Marketing AI Prompts

A ready-to-use library of AI prompts for marketing **Deluxe Group** — generator
supply, rental, and power solutions. Paste any prompt into Claude (or another AI
assistant) and fill in the `[BRACKETED]` placeholders with your own details.

## How to use

1. Copy a prompt from the section you need.
2. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with real details (product, audience, offer, tone).
3. Paste it into the AI tool and generate.
4. Review, edit for accuracy (prices, specs, contact details), then publish.

**Reusable context block** — paste this above any prompt so the AI knows your
business. Edit it once to match reality:

```
Business context:
- Company: Deluxe Group
- What we do: supply, rental, installation, and maintenance of diesel generators
  and power solutions for construction sites, events, industrial, and commercial clients
- Region: [e.g. UAE / GCC]
- Key strengths: [e.g. fast delivery, 24/7 support, genuine parts, competitive rental rates]
- Brands / capacity range: [e.g. 10 kVA to 2000 kVA]
- Tone of voice: professional, reliable, straightforward
```

## Tips for better output

- **Be specific.** Swap generic placeholders for real product names, capacities
  (kVA), locations, and offers — the output quality tracks the detail you give.
- **Set the audience.** "Procurement manager" vs "event organiser" changes tone
  and wording. State it.
- **Ask for options.** Request 3 variations, then pick and refine the best.
- **Always fact-check.** AI can invent prices, specs, or claims. Verify every
  number and promise before publishing.
- **Keep the brand consistent.** Reuse the context block at the top so tone and
  positioning stay uniform across channels.
