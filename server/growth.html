📈 Growth & Direct Sales Plan

Winning more long-term orders from good-paying companies — face to face, not online.

🎯 The goal

More generators out on hire, to reliable paying companies, on long-term contracts. Grow by meeting decision-makers in person at new project sites and companies across the UAE — the way rental business is really won here.

🌅 Your daily routine

1
Morning — fix the schedule. Open Service Plan + Map. Clear today's services, breakdowns and deliveries so the fleet runs clean. 30–45 min.
2
Check what's idle. On the Map, filter Ready to rent. Every Available unit in the yard is money sitting still — those are your push list for today's visits.
3
Visit 3–5 companies / sites. From the target list below + your leads. Meet the right person, leave a card + rate card, log it here.
4
End of day — log every visit in the Leads tracker (stage + next action date). Follow-ups are where orders actually close.

🏗️ Who to target (highest generator demand in the UAE)

Construction & infrastructure
Towers, villas, roads, bridges. Main contractors + subcontractors. Biggest, longest hires.
Oil & gas / energy
ADNOC projects, plants, pipelines, fuel stations (like your new DG-862/562 hire). Good payers.
Events & hospitality
Weddings, concerts, exhibitions, Ramadan tents, festivals. High rates, seasonal.
Industrial / factories
Mussafah, ICAD, KIZAD. Standby + backup power, continuous hires.
Facilities & real estate
Malls, compounds, towers — backup & temporary power during works.
Farms & remote sites
Off-grid farms (like Al Bahyah), camps, telecom towers.
Government / municipality
Road works, public projects, utilities. Slow to start, very reliable payers.
Scrap, metal & logistics yards
ICAD / Mussafah yards (existing customers) — ask them for referrals.

🔎 Where to find new projects & companies

  • Drive the industrial zones — Mussafah, ICAD, KIZAD. Any site with cranes/hoarding = a contractor who needs power. Walk into the site office.
  • Spot new construction — tower cranes, excavation, new hoarding boards (the board names the main contractor — go find them).
  • Project & tender news — MEED Projects, Zawya Projects, Construction Week, Abu Dhabi Municipality & ADNOC tender portals for upcoming UAE projects.
  • Trade shows — The Big 5, ADIPEC (oil & gas), WETEX — meet contractors & buyers in one place.
  • Referrals — ask every happy customer: "who else do you know who needs a generator?" Best & cheapest leads.
  • Event organizers & wedding companies — build a list for the season; repeat business every year.

💬 The pitch (why Deluxe)

  • Reliable, maintained fleet — show the service records & live map; you track every unit professionally.
  • Fast breakdown response — 24/7 teams, nearest-vehicle dispatch (no downtime for the customer).
  • Full range — 45 kVA to 500+ kVA, two companies (Heavy + Energy) — one supplier for all sites.
  • Quick delivery + flexible terms. Competitive monthly rates.
  • Leave a company profile + rate card + business card every visit. Offer a free site survey.

💰 Only chase GOOD-paying companies

Growth means nothing if you don't get paid. Screen before you commit a unit:

  • Prefer — established contractors, large reputable firms, government-backed projects, ADNOC/oil-&-gas.
  • New / unknown customer → take an advance or security deposit, agree clear terms, get the LPO in writing.
  • Use post-dated cheques (PDC) for long hires; agree 30-day payment.
  • Check trade-license age and ask for a trade reference.
  • Red flags — pushes for no deposit, very new tiny company, delays signing the LPO, haggles only on price.
