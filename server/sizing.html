ℹ️ How this is worked out

AC: ~1.2 kW per ton of cooling (compressor + fans). Pump/motor: HP × 0.75 kW.
Running kW → kVA at 0.8 power factor. Then a ×1.3 margin (motor starting inrush, Abu Dhabi heat de-rating, a little spare) — or ×1.5 if everything hard-starts together. Rounded up to the next standard genset.
Aim to load a genset to ~70–80%. A quick guide, not a substitute for a site survey on big or motor-heavy loads.
